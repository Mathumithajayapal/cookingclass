Section 01

Classes & Bookings

Everything you need to know about registering for our culinary masterclasses and modifying your bookings.

No prior experience is necessary! Our masterclasses cater to all skill levels. Instructors break down every technique step-by-step, ensuring absolute beginners and seasoned home cooks both find immense value and joy in the kitchen.
We require a full 48-hour notice prior to your scheduled class for a full refund to your original payment method. Cancellations made within 48 hours will be issued as studio credit to your Student Dashboard for future use.
Yes, everything is fully provided. Each student is assigned a personal workstation equipped with premium Japanese knives, professional induction ranges, pre-measured farm-fresh ingredients, and an embroidered apron for use during the class.
Absolutely. We specialize in private corporate events, birthday parties, and bridal showers. Private events require a minimum of 8 guests and can accommodate up to 24. Please use the "Private Events" tab to request a custom proposal.
Standard masterclasses are restricted to attendees aged 16 and older due to the use of professional equipment and wine pairings. However, we occasionally host dedicated "Family Kitchen" sessions explicitly designed for children and parents.
Yes, we happily accommodate most dietary needs. Please explicitly note any allergies or restrictions (such as vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free requirements) in your Student Dashboard when booking the class so our chefs can prep alternatives.
Section 02

Studio Policies & Experience

Get familiar with our kitchen rules, what to bring, and what happens after the cooking is done.

For safety reasons, closed-toe shoes with non-slip soles are strictly mandatory. We recommend comfortable, casual clothing and suggest tying back long hair. We will provide a chef's apron to protect your clothes while cooking.
Please arrive exactly 15 minutes prior to the scheduled start time. This gives you time to check in at the reception, store your belongings in the lockers, put on your apron, and enjoy a complimentary welcome beverage before the demonstration begins.
Yes! While we host a communal tasting session at the end of class, portions are generous. We provide eco-friendly, biodegradable takeaway containers for any dishes you’d like to bring home to share with your family or friends.
Absolutely. Digital gift vouchers can be purchased directly through our website and emailed to the recipient. Vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase and can be applied to any scheduled class or membership.
Yes. Along with printed recipe cards given during class, digital copies of the recipes, exclusive chef preparation notes, and technique demonstration videos will be permanently unlocked in your online Student Dashboard within 24 hours of completing the class.
We offer 3 hours of validated parking at the central culinary district garage, located just one block east of our studio. Street parking is occasionally available but strictly metered, so we recommend the garage for peace of mind.
